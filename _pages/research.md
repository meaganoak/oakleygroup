# Research in f-Element Electronic Structure
Our research uses correlated quantum chemistry to understand the electronic structure of f-element compounds. We develop and apply advanced computational methods to uncover how electronic structure determines chemical bonding and magnetism. By simulating spectra and comparing directly with experiment, we provide insight into these complex systems while also developing predictive tools for interpreting experimental data.
