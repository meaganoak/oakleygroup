<p>
Our research focuses on the use of correlated quantum chemistry methods for modelling
f-element electronic structure. In particular, we work to understand chemical bonding,
electronic structure, magnetism, and the interpretation of electronic spectra of
lanthanide and actinide systems.
</p>

<h3>Spectroscopy</h3>

<p>
  We develop theoretical approaches for interpreting electronic and magnetic
  spectroscopies of f-element compounds. By combining quantum chemical methods
  with experimental measurements, we gain insight into electronic structure,
  excited states, and the origins of observed spectral behaviour.
</p>

<h3>Chemical Bonding</h3>

<p>
  Our work investigates the nature of chemical bonding in lanthanide and
  actinide systems. Through advanced electronic structure calculations, we
  explore covalency, orbital interactions, and the factors that govern
  bonding across the f-block.
</p>

<h3>Magnetism</h3>

<p>
  We study magnetic anisotropy, spin–orbit coupling, and magnetic exchange
  interactions in molecular f-element compounds. Understanding these effects
  is essential for the design of next-generation magnetic materials and
  molecular quantum technologies.
</p>
