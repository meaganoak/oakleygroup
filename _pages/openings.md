## Undergraduate research

**Interested in an undergraduate research project?**
Please [email me](mailto:meagan.oakley@manchester.ac.uk) with a current CV and brief statement of interest **before January**. We can then discuss potential projects and funding.

### Funding opportunities

- **Departmental & RSC undergraduate research bursaries:** applications open in January. See the [RSC undergraduate research bursaries](https://www.rsc.org/prizes-funding/funding/find-funding/undergraduate-research-bursaries/).

- **Learning Through Research Student Experience Internships:** opportunities through the [University of Manchester](https://www.careers.manchester.ac.uk/findjobs/internships/2ndyear/sei/).

- **Porter Prize:** supports undergraduate research for students who are not yet in their final year. [More information](https://saltersinstitute.org/programmes/awards/porter-prize/).

- **Mitacs Globalink Research Award:** 12–24 week research internships for eligible Canadian students. [More information](https://www.mitacs.ca/our-programs/globalink-research-award/).

## Postgraduate research

**Interested in a PhD in the Oakley Group?**
Please [email me](mailto:meagan.oakley@manchester.ac.uk) with a current CV and brief statement of interest. We can then discuss potential projects and funding.

### Funding opportunities

- **President's Doctoral Scholarship:** [University of Manchester](https://www.se.manchester.ac.uk/study/postgraduate-research/fees-and-funding/search-for-funding/presidents-doctoral-scholarship/).

- **Dean's Doctoral Scholarship:** [University of Manchester](https://www.se.manchester.ac.uk/study/postgraduate-research/fees-and-funding/search-for-funding/deans-doctoral-scholarship/).

- **School of Natural Sciences Diversity PhD Scholarships:** for eligible UK Black-heritage students. [More information](https://www.se.manchester.ac.uk/study/postgraduate-research/fees-and-funding/search-for-funding/natural-sciences-diversity-scholarship/).

- **Postgraduate Research Teaching Associate (PGRTA) Scholarships:** PhD funding combining research and teaching. [More information](https://www.se.manchester.ac.uk/study/postgraduate-research/fees-and-funding/search-for-funding/teaching-associate-scholarship/).

- **Mitacs Globalink Research Award:** 12–24 week research internships for eligible Canadian students. [More information](https://www.mitacs.ca/our-programs/globalink-research-award/).

See the University's [full list of postgraduate funding opportunities](https://www.se.manchester.ac.uk/study/postgraduate-research/fees-and-funding/).

## Postdoctoral researchers

**Interested in joining the Oakley Group as a postdoc?**
There are currently no funded postdoctoral positions in the group, but I am happy to support suitable external fellowship applications. Please [email me](mailto:meagan.oakley@manchester.ac.uk) with a current CV and brief statement of interest.

### External fellowships

- [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships)

- [Banting Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://banting.fellowships-bourses.gc.ca/en/home-accueil.html): Canadian candidates
